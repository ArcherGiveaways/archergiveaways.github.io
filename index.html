DonutSMP Rains & Claims

5M Coins Requirement

To claim rewards from our community rains, your account must have at least 5,000,000 (5M) coins on DonutSMP.

How to Claim

Join our Discord server and navigate to the #claim-rain channel to open a support ticket with staff.

Staff Verification

Once your ticket is open, staff will verify your balance in-game using /coins [username] before paying out your rewards.
